documentation written for BF3 Render Stack. It does not grant rights to any
third-party game material.

The repository intentionally excludes:

- raw `.rdc` RenderDoc captures;

- extracted DICE textures;
- extracted 3D LUT and film-grain resources;
- extracted framebuffer / render-target EXRs;

technical comparison/commentary. Those portions are third-party material and
are not relicensed under MIT.

The executable reconstruction code is independently written from observed GPU
behavior and documented constants.
